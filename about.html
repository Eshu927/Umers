About Paul Interior Imamsahib

Your trusted expert partner in high-precision aluminum, steel, and PVC interior fabrication works located in Shopian.

Our Shop & Business Speciality

Welcome to Paul Interior, situated right in the heart of Imamsahib, Shopian (Jammu & Kashmir). Our workshop and retail establishment serve as the ultimate regional hub for state-of-the-art aluminum sliding windows, heavy-duty stainless steel security gates, custom balcony railings, and luxurious PVC panelling works. We take immense pride in operating a fully equipped fabrication unit where raw metal profiles and fine wood finishes are transformed into breathtaking architectural elements under the direct supervision of expert master craftsmen.

Over the years, our shop has built an unbreakable reputation for honesty, superior craftsmanship, and timely execution. Whether a customer walks in looking for a small residential window repair or a complete multi-storey commercial building facade and false ceiling project, we treat every single undertaking with the highest level of dedication and technical precision.
